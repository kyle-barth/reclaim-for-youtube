Reclaim for YouTube

Something missing on YouTube? Turn this off to check. No reload needed.YouTube is back to normal, except Autoplay and audio on a video that’s already playing.

Paused while Reclaim is off.

Essentials

Shown to ad-block users. Reclaim doesn’t block ads.

Shorts

YouTube default, apart from Subscriptions below.

The Shorts feed and channels’ Shorts tabs.

Shorts links open in the normal player.

Your channels only. No “Most relevant” or Shorts shelves.

Browsing

“First page only” stops endless scrolling. “Subscriptions instead” needs you to be signed in.

Videos you’ve watched 90% of, on Home, Subscriptions and Up next. Needs sign-in with watch history on.

Such as “People also watched” and “For you”. On news searches, “Top news” may go too.

Animated thumbnails too. Unlike YouTube’s “Inline playback”, works signed out and in private windows.

Includes the Premium link in “More from YouTube”.

For Premium, the YouTube app and turning on watch history.

Watch page

Beside the player. Playlists, chat, chapters and transcripts stay.

Uses YouTube’s own switch, which may sync to your account. Turn it back on in the player and that tab keeps it.

Video player

Never hidden: the autoplay countdown, paid-promotion notices, captions and controls.

End-of-video wall, “More videos” on pause, fullscreen grid.

End-screen tiles, info cards and the channel watermark.

Creator-made dubs and your own pick in the player are kept.

Strips the si= and pp= tags from links you copy or share.

Nothing leaves your browser. Changes apply right away.
